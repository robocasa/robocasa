# Tasks

The Unitree G1 benchmark contains **50 tasks: 25 seen atomic, 15 seen composite,
and 10 unseen composite**. The authoritative names, categories, and splits are
in [`robocasa_eval/tasks50.json`](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/2b5bf40398b386cf7e6ebefd7acf7ddf5c559b04/robocasa_eval/tasks50.json). The
paired demonstration release, [`benchmark40-v1-hdf5-20261022`](datasets.md), contains the 40 seen tasks,
with 50 demonstrations per task (2,000 total). The 10 unseen tasks are evaluation
tasks, not additional training demonstrations in that release. “Unseen” is
relative to this 40-task training set; it makes no claim about foundation-model
pretraining.

The standard evaluation horizon is **40 seconds of simulation time for atomic
tasks and 180 seconds for every composite task**, equivalent to 2,000 and 9,000
50 Hz policy steps. These are episode limits, not the policy's predicted action
chunk length. See the [horizon implementation](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/2b5bf40398b386cf7e6ebefd7acf7ddf5c559b04/robocasa_eval/sim_backend.py#L92)
and [evaluation guide](evaluation.md).

## Reading the task table

Each task name links to its environment class; **prompt** links to the actual
`get_ep_meta()` method and **success** to `_check_success()`, including inherited
implementations. Instructions below are current default examples or templates:
`{...}` denotes an episode-specific object, fixture, side, rack, or direction.
The complete instruction for a rollout is `get_ep_meta()["lang"]`; tasks that
support novel instructions may choose a different phrasing. The success column
describes the implemented predicate, including conditions weaker than the prose
instruction. Source was reviewed on **2026-10-29**.

`far(object)` means the current [`gripper_obj_far`](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/2b5bf40398b386cf7e6ebefd7acf7ddf5c559b04/robocasa/utils/object_utils.py#L645)
helper: distance from the **right end-effector site** to the object's body origin
is greater than 0.25 m, unless another threshold is stated. It is not a check
that both hands released the object. “In/on” follows the task's receptacle or
fixture helper rather than a new geometric test. A selected **active burner**
means stove contact, distance to that burner, and its knob being on, as checked
by [`check_obj_location_on_stove`](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/2b5bf40398b386cf7e6ebefd7acf7ddf5c559b04/robocasa/models/fixtures/stove.py#L194).

The last column records G1 adaptations and release-specific details. All rows
use the G1 embodiment and its shared placement/control integration;
task-specific behavior is described alongside each success condition.

## G1 adaptations and naming

- **PortionPeaches** is the canonical name in this release. The task replaces
  the `PortionHotDogs` naming found in older RoboCasa365 catalogs with two peaches
  portioned from a bowl onto two plates. Use `PortionPeaches` in environment names,
  data paths, and result keys; `PortionHotDogs` is not an extra benchmark task.
- **CheesyBread** and **PackDessert** are atomic tasks, each with a 40-second
  limit. **DrawerCakeSort** is one of the 15 seen composites and has a 180-second
  limit. Classification comes from the benchmark manifest, not task-name wording.
- **Navigation** measures the G1 pelvis/base relative to the target *robot base
  placement*, not distance to the fixture surface. Its 0.25 m radius and 0.98 yaw
  cosine both apply. The [source](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/2b5bf40398b386cf7e6ebefd7acf7ddf5c559b04/robocasa/environments/kitchen/atomic/kitchen_navigate.py#L130)
  selects a different body for mobile-base robots.
- **Initial placement** has explicit SonicG1 offsets for `SlideDishwasherRack`
  (0.50 m left) and `CloseFridgeDrawer` (0.05 m backward). These are robot-frame
  offsets, not modified success thresholds; see the
  [offset table](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/2b5bf40398b386cf7e6ebefd7acf7ddf5c559b04/robocasa/utils/env_utils.py#L51) and
  [SonicG1 applicability check](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/2b5bf40398b386cf7e6ebefd7acf7ddf5c559b04/robocasa/utils/env_utils.py#L1356).
  `PickPlaceCounterToStove` also has a
  [SonicG1 source-container placement branch](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/2b5bf40398b386cf7e6ebefd7acf7ddf5c559b04/robocasa/environments/kitchen/atomic/kitchen_pick_place.py#L874).
- **Reach and object configuration** include `DrawerCakeSort`'s 0.15 m outer-edge
  sampling band, `LoadDishwasher`'s 0.15 m leftward dish-region shift, and the
  container/object scales recorded in the table. These are current local
  configurations; rows do not imply that every such setting is G1-only.
- **Dex3 grasp checks** use hand-object contact in the

## Implementation notes

This reference documents the current task behavior:

- `DumpLeftovers` checks both `leftover1` and `leftover2` independently. Both
  must be outside the bowl, the bowl must be inside the sink, and `far(bowl)`
  must hold. Earlier revisions repeated the `leftover1` test; results using
  the corrected scoring must retain their own source revision.
- `OpenDrawer`, `ChooseMeasuringCup`, and `DrawerCakeSort` write the selected
  drawer-side robot anchor into the fresh G1 MJCF before simulation starts.
  Replay using the original saved XML and states retains the recorded spawn.
  Record evaluations of this initialization revision separately from earlier
  runs; see [release provenance](reproducibility.md#scoring-and-drawer-initialization-revision).
- Earlier `PrepareBroilingStation` revisions could sample an initial arrangement
  that already satisfied its native predicate. From commit `ef3c38d`,
  [fresh placement sampling](https://github.com/Xucxname/robocasa/blob/ef3c38de1189d777c1dfcc28f0c78e5bd517c446/robocasa/environments/kitchen/composite/broiling_fish/prepare_broiling_station.py#L80)
  requires the tongs and plate to start at least **0.40 m apart in XY before
  reset settling**. It tries up to 100 candidates, then uses the existing scene
  construction retry if none qualifies. Both objects may still use the same
  counter. Recorded `object_cfgs` metadata and deterministic XML resets bypass
  this new rejection filter to preserve the replay path. The success condition
  remains XY distance **< 0.30 m**, both objects contacting a counter, and the
  right hand far from the tongs; it adds no pickup or movement-history test.
  This changes the fresh initialization distribution. Record evaluations of the
  new source separately from historical results, as described in
  [release provenance](reproducibility.md#task-initialization-revision).
- `LoadDishwasher` currently requires both top-rack contact and a closed door.
- `TiltPan` counts qualifying tilt updates but
  does not separately test that the final pan angle returns near its initial angle.
